## Two real defects found + fixed (beyond infra)
1. **Fixture envelope shape** (root cause of 47 zero-row objects + 30+ STRING_OVERFLOW skips): `gen-fixture` wrapped every list body in a generic `{data:[…], pagination:{…}}` envelope, but real Vanilla returns **bare JSON arrays** with pagination in the RFC-5988 `Link` header. Fixed the fixtures to bare arrays (connector was already correct). Forward went 19/54 → **195/195**.
2. **Article ↔ ArticleRevision FK cycle** (last zero-row object): Article carried a reverse FK `articleRevisionID → ArticleRevision` while ArticleRevision is the *child* (fetched via `/articles/{id}/revisions`). The 2-cycle degraded DAG ordering → `ZERO_PARENTS`. Neutralized the reverse FK (the parent-child nesting is the authoritative ordering edge) in both the DB and the metadata file.
