`VideoPlayout` plays every kind of `RealtimeVideoFrame` through a decoder registry. `/media` adds `VideoFrameDecoderRegistry` (the page's decoders: `Register`, which replaces a decoder of the same name, `Unregister`, and `Candidates(kind, mimeType)`, highest priority first) and the decoder contract (`IVideoFrameDecoder`, `VideoFrameDecoderRegistration` with a synchronous `CanPlay`, `VideoFrameDecoderContext`). Three decoders are built in: `mse-fmp4` (today's MSE code, moved unchanged), `webcodecs` (encoded chunks through a WebCodecs `VideoDecoder`, configured at the first key frame once `isConfigSupported` confirms the codec; H.264 in Annex B with the codec string from the MIME type's `codecs` parameter, VP8, VP9, AV1) and `image` (JPEG, PNG and WebP through `createImageBitmap`). Chunks and images show at their `PresentationTimeMs`, paced on the wall clock, in the host's `<video>` element through a `MediaStream` (a track generator in Chromium, else a canvas captured at 0 fps), so the tile, its stall watch and the hosts are unchanged; the last picture holds, and an element attached later is shown it. The player keeps one decoder for each frame type (kind and MIME essence), starts the one for its configured type when an element is attached, and falls back to the next decoder for a type when one gives up (a browser that can't decode the codec, or three decode errors within a minute); a type no decoder plays is dropped and reported once (`'no-decoder'`). `VideoPlayout.IsSupported(mimeType)` asks the registry. No new problem kinds: a stream that starts on a delta chunk reports `'fragment-before-init'`.
