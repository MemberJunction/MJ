Gemini avatar sessions no longer take the idle video Vertex AI streams between answers as the agent speaking (#5312). Every streamed piece used to mark the model busy and, in the browser, the agent `'speaking'`: after an answer, the next idle piece held context notes, spoken updates and blocking tool results until the next `turnComplete`, finalized the user's transcript early, and kept `IsAudioPlaying` true; in a meeting it made the bridge's next commit look like a duplicate. Now the model's answer runs from its first transcribed words, or a PCM voice part that plays, until its `generationComplete`, the turn's `turnComplete`, a barge-in or cancel, a tool call, or a resume. Video inside the answer is the answer's; video outside it is idle, and still plays (the avatar keeps its face) but marks nothing. `GeminiAvatarOutput.Accept` returns `'answer'`, `'idle'` or `'dropped'`, and the browser client marks generation started only for the answer's parts; the bridged session's `responseActive` follows the same answer through the bridged avatar output's new `OnAnswer` sink. `IsAudioPlaying` counts the avatar only while some of an answer's media is still ahead of the playhead: `IAvatarVideoPlayout` and `IVideoFrameDecoder` gain `FramesAhead`, the frames handed over that are still to play (the MSE decoder records where each appended piece ends; the WebCodecs and image decoders count their queues). Usage counting is unchanged. The video conformance kit's VC13 adds the idle video after a turn, for harnesses that implement the new optional `AnswerStarted`, and its recording player gains `FramesAhead`.
