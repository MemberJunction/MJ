Gemini Live sessions no longer cut an answer, or lose a question, when they move to a new connection (#5354, #5358, #5313). On Vertex AI a `goAway`, or a planned move after the first answer, moved the session at once: the resumption helper waited only for Google's `resumable: false`, which Vertex AI never sends. `RealtimeSessionResumption` now takes the turns from the driver (`TurnStarted`, `TurnEnded`). From the user's first transcribed words, a turn the client sends, or the model's first output, until that turn's `turnComplete` with no tool call pending, a planned move waits; it happens at the first resumable handle Google issues after the turn ended. The deadline still bounds the wait, and its margin is now a third of the notice, between 1.5 s and 10 s, so a turn that ends within a short notice finishes on the old connection (a notice of 10 s or less used to move at once). Both Gemini drivers, the browser client and the server-bridged session (and so Gemini Enterprise), put a connection to use only once Google confirms its setup (`setupComplete`, through the new `RealtimeConnectionSetup`), since Google drops input that arrives earlier: the browser client reports `'connected'` and `'listening'` and starts the microphone after it, and a bridged session's `StartSession` resolves after it; either fails with the close code when the connection closes first, or after 15 s without a confirmation. During a move, sends queue and the latest 2 s of input audio are held, and both go out on the new connection once it is ready. The turn state is readable as `IsTurnOpen` on `GeminiRealtimeClient` and on the bridged session.
