MJAPI realtime relay. `RealtimeProxyServer` also serves `/realtime/relay/<ticket>/…` (any suffix after the ticket, which a provider's browser SDK appends): a ticketed websocket that runs a provider's frame policy (`IRealtimeRelayPolicy` in `@memberjunction/ai`) between the browser and the upstream. A relay session (`RealtimeProxyRegistry.IssueRelaySession`) opens one fresh connection within 5 minutes of the mint, then only resumes that present a resumption handle the relay forwarded to that session; it ends at 30 minutes or `MaxSessionSeconds`, whichever comes first, or after 10 connections. The policy mints the upstream headers on every open, writes the opening frames, filters client frames and reports handles; server frames pass unchanged. The relay pings both legs every 20 s and terminates a leg that answers none of its last 2 pings (the other leg gets 1001 upstream or 1014 in the browser), closes a browser that falls more than 16 MiB behind (1013; the client resumes), closes on a client frame over 2 MiB, waits 10 s for the first client frame, keeps the 15 s upstream deadline and the 512-frame pre-open buffer, passes close codes through, keeps one live upstream per session, applies the Origin allowlist, and logs only counts, codes and an 8-character ticket prefix. Relay state is in memory, so a resume must reach the MJAPI instance that issued the session. The MJAPI public-URL helper moves from the HuggingFace driver to `@memberjunction/ai` (`ResolveRealtimeProxyBaseWsUrl`, `HttpOriginToWs`, `BuildRealtimeRelayUrl`); HuggingFace behaves as before. Nothing issues relay sessions yet, and `/realtime-proxy?ticket=` is unchanged.
