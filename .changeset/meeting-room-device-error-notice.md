The meeting room says when a share, camera, microphone or the room's sound fails while it is connected. Before, these errors reached only the room's connection overlay, which is hidden while connected, so a share the browser refused looked like a click that did nothing. `<mj-livekit-agent-room>` shows the latest device error as a dismissible error notice in its notice stack, after the notice about what the agent sees, worded as "Couldn't <what the user asked for>: <the reason given>": "Couldn't start sharing: To share only the panel, choose this tab in the browser's picker." for a "This panel" share where the user picked another surface, "Couldn't turn on your camera: Could not start video source" for a camera that won't start. A share picker the user closes is still no error and shows nothing. The notice goes when the user dismisses it, asks for another change to their devices (the room's `BeforeMediaToggle` or `BeforeDeviceSwitch`), leaves, or joins again; device errors before the room connects are left to its connection overlay, and every error is still re-emitted on `ErrorOccurred`. In `livekit-room-core`, a `device` error says which device it is about and what the user asked of it: `LiveKitRoomError.Device`, a new `LiveKitErrorDevice` with `Media` (`'microphone' | 'camera' | 'screen' | 'speaker'`) and `Change` (`'on' | 'off' | 'switch'`, absent when the room can't tell). `LiveKitRoomController` sets it when turning the microphone, camera or share on or off, switching a device, or starting the room's sound fails, and on LiveKit's `MediaDevicesError` (the device kind only); the preview room sets it for a share or a camera or microphone that fails to start. A device error about no one device (listing the devices) has none. Error messages are unchanged.
