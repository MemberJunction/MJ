A meeting now says why an agent's avatar isn't shown when the session prep decided it. A server-side (bridged) session reported only the prep's `phone` reason: when the prep asked the driver for no avatar because the voiced agent's persona has no face for the model's vendor (`no-binding`), the agent names an avatar the vendor doesn't have (`unknown-avatar`), or the model's Video/Output row turns video off (`endpoint`), the driver reported nothing, so the LiveKit room's bot joined without `mj.agentAvatar` and the room showed the agent's voice with no notice. `WireBridgeRealtimeSession` now gives such a session the status its prep decided: `phone` first; then `bridged` when the host publishes no avatar into a room, which is what the driver says when asked there (the room names its own reason, such as `decoder-missing`); then `endpoint` when the model shows no avatar, as a browser call says; else the prep's reason. The bot joins with `mj.agentAvatar` `audio-only:<reason>` and the room shows its existing notice, such as "Audio only for {Agent}: this agent has no avatar for this voice model". A session whose prep asked the driver for an avatar keeps the driver's status, so a granted avatar is unchanged. Core's `IRealtimeSession.AvatarStatus` documentation names the reasons the prep reports.
