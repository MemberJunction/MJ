Two login Access drivers open a cookie session for a members-only site, with the user name and password from MJ: Credentials (mapped by `Configuration.AccessCredentials`, as for `Credential`) and the rest from the source's settings.

`FormLogin` posts the login form through `network-utils`' `HttpRequest` with the SSRF guard on every hop (`HttpResponse.SetCookies` keeps each `Set-Cookie` header on its own; `SafeFetch` / `HttpRequest` gain an `OnRedirect` hook that sees each redirect and may change the next request), keeping cookies in a `tough-cookie` jar (RFC 6265 matching, public suffixes refused) (optionally after a GET of the login page for its cookies and hidden fields, as a form or JSON, with `LoginUsernameField` / `LoginPasswordField` / `LoginExtraFields`, and `LoginSuccessMarker` / `LoginFailureMarker`), following redirects through the SSRF guard and keeping their cookies. `BrowserLogin` drives the login page in the Headless renderer's browser (pre-click, user name, continue, password, submit, confirm, success selector, cookie marker). Both return the session for `SessionOrigins` (default the source's origin) as one `Cookie` header per origin in the new `AccessArtifact.OriginHeaders` (read with `AccessHeadersFor` in content-pipeline-base; every consumer of a session uses it), so a host-only or `Secure` cookie never reaches another origin and same-named cookies of different origins both survive; it expires with the first cookie or `SessionTtlMinutes`. A FormLogin seed GET that answers an error fails the login before anything is posted; a relative `LoginActionURL` resolves against the seed's final URL; a login that runs out of time reports a timeout. `BrowserLogin` checks every redirect hop against the SSRF guard (a redirected navigation becomes a checked navigation; no request that still carries its body, navigation or script fetch, is followed by a 307/308 to another origin, so credentials are never re-posted to another site; a hop to another origin drops the browser's cookie and authorization headers; other requests' redirects are followed hop by hop) and refuses WebSockets, which request routing does not see. `LoginCookieMarker` is checked against the cookies that go to the session's origins; a login whose session lives only in `localStorage` is accepted; an already-cancelled request starts no browser. It also puts the browser's storage state, cut to the session's origins (their cookies and `localStorage`; an identity provider's own cookies stay behind), in `AccessArtifact.Extensions.StorageState` (`SessionStorageState` in content-pipeline-base), and the `Headless` renderer restores it, cookies and localStorage, into each page on the session's origins: the cookies host-only, for the access hosts only. The renderer also forwards no cookie or authorization header on a subresource redirect to another origin, keeps settling (`WaitSelector`, `ScrollToBottom`) within `TimeoutMs` and the cancel signal (a `WaitSelectorTimeoutMs` of 0 or less means the default), and reads a page with no `<body>`. `SafeFetch` releases a redirect's body before calling `OnRedirect` (no socket is held when the hook refuses), and headers `OnRedirect` returns are kept under `StripHeadersOnCrossOriginRedirect`. The server-bootstrap class manifest registers both drivers.
