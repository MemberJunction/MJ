A conformance kit for realtime video providers. The new `@memberjunction/ai-realtime-client/testing` subpath exports `RunRealtimeVideoConformance(driverFactory)`, which runs 17 checks (`VC01`-`VC15`, `VF01`-`VF02`) against a provider's client driver and resolves to a result per check (Passed, Failed with the rule, or Skipped with the reason), and `ListRealtimeVideoConformanceChecks(driverFactory)`, which gives a test runner one test per check. The kit needs no test framework. A provider writes a harness (`IRealtimeVideoConformanceHarness`): what its server mints, its client driver with a fake transport and its creation seams wired to the kit's recorders (`RecordingVideoPlayout`, and `RecordingPcmPlayback`, which is also a playback clock), and the model's side of the wire, its video as Core's `RealtimeVideoFrame`s (fragmented MP4, encoded chunks or images; traits `VideoFrameKind`, `TimedVoice`). The checks cover what a session hands the host (an audio-only session no video, a granted avatar once, none when the host doesn't show it or the browser can't play it), where the model's parts go (video to its player in order and typed as sent, never to the voice; an unknown type nowhere, reported once), barge-in, turn ends, a video that carries the voice, a timed voice as the player's clock, generated video seconds, resumes and Disconnect. Fixtures include `ConformanceChunkFrames` and `ConformanceImageFrame`. The package's tests run it against Gemini (Developer, and Enterprise on a relay session), OpenAI, and a synthetic provider built only on the package's public API (fMP4, chunk and image runs). The main entry and `/media` don't export the kit. `BaseRealtimeClient`'s driver obligations gain #12 (the agent's video keeps the voice's rules). In `@memberjunction/ai-agents`, `GetRealtimeModelVoices` marks a persona voice with its avatar (`AvatarID`, which the picker shows as "Comes with an avatar") only when the model shows avatars on that vendor (`RealtimeModelShowsAvatar`: its Video/Output row and its driver's endpoint profile).
