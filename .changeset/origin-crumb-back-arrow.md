A record page's origin crumb now has a labeled "Back" button, separated from the breadcrumb by a divider, that returns to where the record was opened (the same full restore as the page segment). Its tooltip names the destination, e.g. "Back to Entities". The old back arrow was a decorative icon next to the first segment, so clicking it did nothing.
