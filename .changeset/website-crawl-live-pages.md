The website crawl behind `AutotagWebsite.Discover` reports only pages it has fetched and found alive, and reads sites politely.

A link is now fetched before it is reported: a lasting client error (a 4xx other than 408, 425 and 429) is remembered as dead and never becomes an item; a Cloudflare challenge (`cf-mitigated`), a server error or no answer is tried once more at the end of the queue and then reported for Extract to decide; a file that is not HTML is reported without its links being read. The pages one level past `MaxDepth` are fetched (to know they are alive) but not read for links. `MaxPages` keeps its meaning, the most pages whose links are read: once it is reached the pages already found are still checked and reported, unread, and dead pages, files and retries do not count. A page's `Last-Modified` (read from the crawl's own fetch) now filters it against the walk's `Since`, as a sitemap `lastmod` already did; the separate HEAD request per page that did this before is gone (a rendered page keeps the `Last-Modified` of the HEAD or plain fetch before it). `Website.CheckLeafPages: false` reports the pages whose links will not be read unfetched, as before, saving the crawl one request per page at the cost of dead links becoming items. The resume token is now version 2; a version-1 token restarts the walk from the start page.

Politeness: each host has its own rate limiter; a host that answers 429 (or 503 with Retry-After) is slowed and the fetch retried after the longer of what it asks and an exponential backoff; a page still limited goes to the back of the queue rather than losing its links (`Configuration.Website.RateLimit`: `MaxRetries` 3, `BackoffMs` 2000, `MaxBackoffMs` 60000, `RequestsPerSecond` 10, `MinRequestsPerSecond` 0.5).

`RetryAfter` (`ParseRetryAfterValue`, `ExtractRetryAfterFromError`), `RetryRunner` (`WithRetry`, which gains a `Sleep` hook) and `RateLimiter` move from `@memberjunction/integration-engine` to `@memberjunction/network-utils`, unchanged otherwise, so the crawl shares them; integration-engine now imports them from there and **no longer exports them: code that imported them from `@memberjunction/integration-engine` must import them from `@memberjunction/network-utils`**. `Website.Concurrency` (default 1) fetches that many pages at a time.

New, all off by default: `Website.MergeWwwAndScheme`, `StripTrackingParams` / `StripQueryParams`, `DedupCanonical`, `DedupRedirects`, `LoopLimit`, `SkipCalendarPaths`, `IncludePatterns` / `ExcludePatterns` (substrings, or `re:` regular expressions; the start page is always visited for its links), `UserAgent` (default `Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; MemberJunction/1.0)`, for the crawl's and Extract's plain requests; before, they sent the runtime's own) and `MaxResponseBytes`. A page's body that has not finished arriving 30 s after its headers, or when the walk is cancelled, is given up rather than read forever. `network-utils` gains `HttpRequestConfig.UserAgent` and `MaxResponseBytes` (refused while reading, `HttpError.IsTooLarge`; an error answer past it keeps its status, with no body). A sitemap is read up to the protocol's 50 MB rather than `MaxResponseBytes`, and a sitemap that cannot be read is logged. The options are documented on `IContentSourceConfiguration` (metadata JSONType); the generated `MJContentSourceEntity_IContentSource*` types are regenerated with them. To change the crawl, subclass: `AutotagWebsite.CreateCrawl` / `CreateCrawlFetchers`, and `WebsiteCrawl.ParsePage` / `CreateScope` / `CreateRateLimiter` are protected extension points.

Safety and order: a URL the SSRF guard refuses is dropped (never rendered, retried or reported); a link that redirects off the site or onto an excluded page is not reported; `RootURL` is matched by origin and path segment (`/docs` does not cover `/docs-private`, and a look-alike host or another port is outside); robots.txt- and index-named sitemaps are read only on the start page's site, an HTML answer is not a sitemap, and sitemap reading resumes across parts. In `Always` mode a HEAD answering 429, 503 with Retry-After, or a challenge backs off instead of rendering; a failed render keeps the plain fetch's `retry-after` / `cf-mitigated`; a host's pause is capped at `MaxBackoffMs`, and only a real answer counts as a success for the rate limiter. The crawl's settings follow the pipeline's order: run, source `Configuration`, `SourceSpecificConfiguration` (its flat crawl keys included), source type, then the type's declared defaults. Each walk carries its own request, so overlapping walks on one driver never share a session or drop the SSRF guard. `MaxPages` of 0 or less means no limit; a duplicate page (`DedupCanonical` / `DedupRedirects`) does not count toward it. Login sessions' per-origin cookies (`AccessArtifact.OriginHeaders`) are sent by the crawl and by the RSS feed driver only to their own origin.
