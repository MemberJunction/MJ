A meeting now says why an agent's avatar isn't shown when the model's driver renders no avatar. When the voiced agent's persona has a face (a Video binding) on a vendor whose realtime driver renders none, the session prep asks that driver for the avatar, and every driver except Gemini's ignores the request and reports no `AvatarStatus`. A bridged session then had no status, the LiveKit room's bot joined without `mj.agentAvatar`, and the room showed no notice, where a browser call in the same case says `endpoint`. `WireBridgeRealtimeSession` (`ResolveBridgedAvatarStatus`) now gives a session whose driver was asked for an avatar and reported nothing a status: `bridged` when the host publishes no avatar into a room (the room then names its own reason, such as `decoder-missing`), else `endpoint` when the model shows no avatar (`RealtimeModelShowsAvatar`: its Video/Output row and the driver's `SupportsAvatarOutput`). The bot joins with `mj.agentAvatar` `audio-only:endpoint`, and the room shows its existing notice, "Audio only for {Agent}: this voice model can't show an avatar". A status the driver reported (a grant, or one of the Gemini driver's reasons) is unchanged, and a driver whose model shows avatars and reports nothing still gets no status. Core's documentation of `IRealtimeSession.AvatarStatus` and `BaseRealtimeModel.SupportsAvatarOutput` says so.
