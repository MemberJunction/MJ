Content pipeline: a Content Item's `EmbeddingStatus` now follows its chunks. Embed runs over chunks, so until now an item stayed at the `Pending` that Extract set, even after every chunk was embedded, and anything that read the item to ask "is this document embedded?" got the wrong answer. After each Embed page the pipeline reads the live chunks of the items that page touched and, once none has embedding work left, sets the item: `Failed` if any chunk failed, `Skipped` if every chunk was skipped, `Complete` (with `LastEmbeddedAt`) otherwise. An item Segment is due to cut again, or one being deleted, is left alone. When Segment ends an item without chunks (it failed, or there was nothing to cut) the item's `EmbeddingStatus` takes the same value in that commit. A new `SettleItemEmbedding` stage and the `Content Pipeline — Settle Item Embedding` Record Process settle an item an Embed page missed (a process stopped between committing the chunks and settling the item). `RollUpItemEmbedding` and `ItemEmbeddingRollup` are exported. Because items now reach `Complete`, a metadata change marks the item `MetadataOnly` along with its chunks, and the rollup returns it to `Complete` once they are refreshed.
