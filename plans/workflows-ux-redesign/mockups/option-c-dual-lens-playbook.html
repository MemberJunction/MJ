OPTION C Dual-Lens Playbook & Guided Diagnostic Co-Pilot
Design Philosophy
Readable as a Playbook, Inspectable as a Pathway, Guided by AI. Inspired by Relay.app, Zapier Central, Notion Automations, and Observable: every workflow lives in a synchronized Structured Playbook Outline ⇄ Stage/Wave Pathway Diagram with inline variable pills, Person approvals front-and-center, and plain-English diagnostic guidance.
Best For
Mixed citizen-builder + engineering organizations where VP Ops / domain experts author, review, and approve workflows alongside staff engineers who still want topological clarity for parallel waves, AI Decisions, Saga rollbacks, and time-travel debugging.
Key Tradeoff
Structured Playbook + Wave-Lane layout prioritizes instant readability, zero-JSON authoring, and guided recovery over freeform 2D node dragging (auto-structured stages take precedence over manual pixel coordinates).
