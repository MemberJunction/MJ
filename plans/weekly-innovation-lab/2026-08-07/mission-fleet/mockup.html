Mission Fleet

Free, open, ready-to-run AI crew for associations and nonprofits. No contract, no data-science team required — install one, try it on sample data, decide if it earns a place on your real data.

Open-source · MIT licensed · no vendor lock-in

Every crew agent declares exactly what it reads and writes before you install it. Pause or remove any agent at any time — your data never leaves your own MJ instance.

Portable by design

Renewal Concierge

Membership & dues renewal

Flags at-risk renewals early and drafts personalized outreach — not a blast, a reason. Works standalone; gets sharper if Belonging Radar is also installed.

Ready standalone+ Belonging Radar (optional)
Touches: reads Membership & Renewal records · drafts (never sends) outreach for review.

Grant Scout

Funding & compliance

Surfaces relevant funding opportunities and extracts grant obligations into a trackable deadline list, so nothing slips.

Needs Impact Ledger
Touches: reads uploaded grant documents · writes Grant Obligation records · never submits applications.

Event Crew Chief

Volunteer staffing

Matches volunteers to open shifts by skill and availability, and flags staffing gaps before an event, not during it.

Needs Belonging Radar
Touches: reads volunteer profiles & shift openings · drafts invitations for coordinator review.

Community Voice

Newsletter & social drafting

Turns approved Impact Moments into newsletter and social copy in your organization's voice — draft only, always reviewed before publishing.

Needs Impact Ledger
Touches: reads approved Impact Moments · drafts content for review · never posts on its own.
