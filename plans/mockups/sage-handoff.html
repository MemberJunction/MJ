Sequence

  1. Sage ends its turn with a Handoff step. Its message shrinks to one or two plain sentences and the transfer card renders under it.
  2. The server starts the target as a top-level run on a new detail (ParentID = Sage's detail, AgentID = target). While it spins up, the card animates and the target shows a "Joining" skeleton.
  3. The target's reply streams below the card, joined to it by a short rail in the target's color, with "via Sage", its own power level, and its own run clock.
  4. The composer re-targets. "Replying to Video Studio" until the user picks Return to Sage, @mentions Sage, or the target hands back.

Components

mj-agent-handoff-card
New, in Generic/conversations (L2, no Router). Inputs: SourceAgent, TargetAgent, PresetName, Brief, Reason, Status ('Transferring' | 'Transferred' | 'Failed'), HopChain. Brief is collapsed by default.
message-item
Gains a HandedOffFrom agent: renders "via Sage" and the rail. Sage's handed-off message renders in secondary text.
Composer routing chip
Makes the existing continuity routing visible. "Return to Sage" sets the next message's agent back to Sage.
Chat header chip
Shows who you're talking to now; animates its color when ownership changes.

States

  • Transferring: a spark travels Sage → target along the gradient track (1.25 s ease); target avatar pulses on arrival.
  • Transferred: the track is static; the card stays in the thread as a permanent record of the hand-off.
  • Failed: danger stripe replaces the gradient, the reason is stated (permission, not discoverable, not allowed in this app, hop limit), and Sage continues in the same turn.
  • Multi-hop: the card shows a chain, e.g. Sage → Research Agent → Video Studio, collapsed to the last two hops with a "+1" count.

Tokens, motion, access

  • Colors only from --mj-* tokens. Source and target accents come from each agent's color, falling back to --mj-brand-primary; the gradient border uses color-mix() on both.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: no travel or pulse; a static spark marks the direction.
  • The card is role="status" with aria-live="polite", so screen readers hear "Transferred to Video Studio".
  • Works in Slack/Teams as text: "Sage transferred you to Video Studio (High)", then the target's reply.
