## Exceptions taken

Two departures from the standing rules, both ruled by the certification owner and recorded here so the label keeps meaning something.

1. **[#4512](https://github.com/MemberJunction/MJ/pull/4512) — Gemini 3.8 Live + Extended Thinking, backported as a one-off exception to §5.3.** §5.3 admits security, data loss or corruption, a non-functioning deployment, and cert-blockers; a new model integration is none of those and would normally queue for the next line. It was taken as an explicitly one-off exception, not a precedent, and it shipped in the 6.1.2 batch alongside the metadata-sync migration ([#4496](https://github.com/MemberJunction/MJ/pull/4496)/[#4546](https://github.com/MemberJunction/MJ/pull/4546), which covers #4474 and #4512 with guarded creates).
2. **[#4575](https://github.com/MemberJunction/MJ/issues/4575) — the `security-exception` schema migration in 6.1.3.** §12 reserves schema DDL on a certified line for security-driven necessity only, additive under the Publish-No-Break policy, next-first, and byte-identical. [#4583](https://github.com/MemberJunction/MJ/pull/4583) meets that: the migration ships as `migrations/v6/V202609191819__v6.2.x__TaskGraph_Guarded_Write_Sprocs.sql` on both `next` and `lts/6.1`, keeping its `v6.2.x` filename so a 6.1 database upgrading past it later skips it cleanly instead of running it twice. This is what moves the 6.1.3 ledger entry to `dbImpact: schema`, and it is the reason the gate-4 rerun needs a least-privilege leg.
