# Announcement Guidance

## Purpose
Use the Announcement archetype for 15 to 45 second organizational updates, event invitations, milestone notices, or policy changes delivered in widescreen (`16:9`) and vertical (`9:16`) formats. It prioritizes immediate news value: what is changing, who it affects, and when it takes effect.

## Beat Intent
- **hook**: Announce the headline news in the first two to three seconds with unmistakable typographic hierarchy.
- **what-changed**: Explain the core update, event, or milestone in one focused visual card.
- **impact**: Clarify who is affected, key dates, or what changes in day-to-day workflows.
- **close**: Present the effective date, resource location, or required action on a clean closing card with a two-second hold.

## What to Avoid
- Never bury the headline announcement after a slow logo reveal or generic atmospheric intro.
- Avoid cramming full policy memos onto the screen; distill to essential dates and actions while narration provides context.
- Do not use celebratory stock imagery of people or unverified partner logos.

## Facts Rule
Never invent dates, version numbers, metrics, partner logos, product screens, or people. All factual claims and dates must come from the caller's brief or `DataBindings`, and all logos or screenshots must come from `Assets.Approved`. If key announcement details are missing, ask the caller rather than guessing.
