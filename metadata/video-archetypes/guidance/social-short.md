# Social Short Guidance

## Purpose
Use the Social Short archetype for 10 to 30 second vertical (`9:16`) films built for mobile feeds where attention is decided in the first two seconds and many viewers watch with sound off. Composition must use bold, centered-safe vertical framing and burned-in or high-contrast kinetic copy.

## Beat Intent
- **hook**: Stop the scroll within the first two seconds using a bold visual contrast and a five-to-seven-word headline in the upper-middle safe zone.
- **turn**: Deliver the core insight, demonstration, or before-and-after shift with fast, purposeful visual state changes every two to three seconds.
- **payoff**: Show the concrete result or takeaway in a single high-contrast frame that reads instantly on a phone screen.
- **close**: Hold the closing takeaway or call-to-action card inside the vertical safe area away from platform UI overlays.

## What to Avoid
- Never place essential text or controls in the bottom 20% or top 12% of a `9:16` frame where social platform chrome covers the picture.
- Avoid horizontal multi-column layouts in vertical video; stack elements vertically with generous type scale.
- Do not rely on audio alone to carry the message; on-screen text must make complete sense when muted.

## Facts Rule
Never invent numbers, customer quotes, product interfaces, logos, or people. Any metric, screenshot, or brand mark shown in a short must come from `DataBindings` or `Assets.Approved`. If a required asset or fact is absent, ask the caller rather than inventing a substitute.
