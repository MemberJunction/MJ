# Topic Explainer Guidance

## Purpose
Use the Topic Explainer archetype when the viewer needs to understand how a concept, architecture, or process works from end to end within 60 to 120 seconds. It prioritizes clarity, progressive disclosure, and visual cause-and-effect over hype.

## Beat Intent
- **hook**: Open immediately with the core question or misconception the viewer holds, pairing a single headline with the central diagram or mechanism already in motion.
- **context**: Establish why this topic matters right now and what breaks or stays opaque when teams misunderstand it.
- **mechanism**: Walk through the primary system or workflow step by step, revealing one node or state transition at a time so the eye always knows where to look.
- **example**: Ground the abstract mechanism in a concrete scenario showing input, transformation, and outcome side by side.
- **close**: Restate the one-sentence takeaway on a clean summary card and hold long enough for the viewer to absorb the conclusion.

## What to Avoid
- Never dump a full multi-node architecture diagram onto the screen all at once without progressive reveal.
- Avoid decorative motion that competes with the active step being explained by the narration.
- Do not pack full paragraphs into cards; keep on-screen labels to short phrases while the voiceover carries explanatory nuance.

## Facts Rule
Never invent numbers, benchmarks, product screens, logos, or people. Every statistic or metric must come from a resolved `DataBinding`, and every product UI or brand mark must come from an approved asset. If a required fact or visual is missing, stop and ask rather than fabricating a stand-in.
